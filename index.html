Takeaway

In these clips, 720p to 1080p is the most accurate and useful step up: it presents the source more cleanly at a larger display size while retaining its natural character. The 1440p and 4K versions offer more display pixels, but they cannot recreate detail that was not present in the 720p Seedance 2.5 original.

Seedance 2.5 generated the original 720p videos. Upscales use Lanczos resizing with CAS balanced sharpening (0.35), H.264, and the original 24 fps audio.
